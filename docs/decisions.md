Plain text in the Reason and Chosen against columns is the owner's; *italics* are the author's summary where the interview did not state one.

Rows from D58 on carry a Meta column (date, authority, evidence, reversibility) whose keywords are explained in `templates/decisions.md`; older rows leave it blank.
